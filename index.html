My Experience

A Recount Text

A story about my experience designing a UI/UX website using Figma and learning through the process.

Orientation

Hello, my name is Salsabila Nur Aisyah, a student of SMK Negeri 1 Tengaran. On April 13, a collaboration project was started. The first stage of the project was creating a UI/UX design using Figma. At first, I felt quite confused because I did not fully understand how to use Figma properly. Therefore, I searched for design references and watched several tutorials to better understand how to use Figma.

Events

After understanding the basics, I started designing the website from the navbar, hero, about, skill, project, contact, until the footer section. I tried to make the design look modern and neat.

During the design process, I had some difficulties, especially in choosing a suitable 3D design for the hero section and selecting fonts that matched the website theme. Finally, I used 3D designs from Spline and chose Poppins and Playfair fonts because they looked more elegant.

I also consulted my design process with my teacher to get suggestions. My teacher suggested adding gradient colors to make the website look more attractive. After revising several parts, the design was finally finished and printed.

Reorientation

From this experience, I learned many new things about UI/UX design and using Figma. At first, I felt confused, but after practicing and searching for references, designing became easier and more enjoyable.
